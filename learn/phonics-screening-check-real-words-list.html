The other half of every phonics screening check is real words, from chip and jump to portrait and smoother. Here are all 260 from the 13 published papers (2012 to 2026), grouped by year. Some come back again and again, so there are 244 different ones.

Children don't need to know what a word means to read it in the check, but real words are a chance to talk about meaning too: what is a turnip, a tailor, a trapeze?

Questions parents ask

How many real words are in the phonics screening check?

20 of the 40 words are real words. They are words 13 to 20 and 29 to 40.

Will these exact real words be in next year's check?

Very unlikely. Most words are new each year, and the check is designed so that memorising words gives no advantage. Use these lists to practise the sounds, not the words.

Does my child need to know what the real words mean?

No. The check only measures whether a child can read the word aloud correctly. Talking about the meanings is still worthwhile for vocabulary.
