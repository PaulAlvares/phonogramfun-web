The Year 1 phonics check in South Australia

South Australia was the first state to run a Year 1 phonics check, in public schools since 2018. What it involves, how SA children have done, and how to practise at home.

South Australia led the country: its public schools have run the Year 1 phonics screening check since 2018, after a trial in 2017. The department's 2025 results fact sheet says it is "generally reasonable to expect year 1 students to decode 28 out of 40 words", and in 2025, 66% of the 11,350 students who took part, across 413 schools, reached that level.

That's down from 70% in 2024, but well up from 43% in 2018, the first year. In other words, around one Year 1 child in three in SA public schools isn't there yet, which is exactly who the check is designed to find.

What's in the check

Forty words, read aloud one at a time to the teacher. About half are real words (chip, stair, turnip) and half are made-up words (vap, jigh, splote) that a child can only read by sounding out. Made-up words are usually shown with a picture of an imaginary creature, so children know the word is a name and not one they should recognise. The words get harder through the booklet: single sounds first, then letter teams like sh and ee, then vowel teams, blends and two-syllable words. There's more on the made-up words in Alien words, explained.

Twenty practice words

The Australian check is modelled on England's, and England publishes its papers. These are the 20 words from Section 1 of England's 2023 paper, 12 alien then 8 real, the same shape as the first half of the South Australia check. How to help at home

  1. Ten minutes, most days. Short and regular beats long and occasional. End on a word they can read.
  2. Pure sounds. Say /m/ not "muh". Clipped sounds blend; sounds with an "uh" on the end don't.
  3. Read every sound, left to right. The most common mistake on made-up words is guessing a real word from the first letter (reading strom as storm).
  4. Find the gaps. Use the twenty practice words above or a full past paper, then practise only the sounds that went wrong.

Questions parents ask

When did South Australia start the phonics check?

In public schools in 2018, after a trial in 2017. It was the first Australian state to use it.

What is the expected score in the SA phonics check?

28 out of 40. In 2025, 66% of Year 1 students in SA public schools reached it.

When is the phonics check in South Australia?

In Term 3. In 2025 the window ran from 4 to 29 August.

Can I get the actual phonics check words?

No, the Australian check words are confidential. England's past papers, which the Australian check is modelled on, are published, and they make good practice.
