How many phonograms are there?

Spalding counts 70, Logic of English 75, and other programs vary. What a phonogram is, why the numbers differ, and the full list of Spalding's 70.

It depends on who's counting. The Spalding method uses 70 phonograms. Logic of English teaches 75 basic phonograms (plus 48 advanced ones). Other programs use slightly different counts again. They're all describing the same English spelling system; they just draw the lines in slightly different places.

A phonogram is a letter or group of letters that stands for a sound: t, sh, igh. English has about 44 sounds (phonemes) but many more ways of writing them, so any phonogram list is a choice about which spellings are common enough to teach as a unit. More on the basics in What is a phonogram?.

The main counts

ProgramPhonogramsNotes
Spalding (The Writing Road to Reading)7026 single letters, then 44 letter teams, in a fixed teaching order
Logic of English75 basic + 48 advancedAdds some teams Spalding treats as letter combinations
Other phonics and spelling programsVariesOften close to 70, with a few teams added or left out, such as tch

Sources: Great Hearts Academies, Spalding phonograms ("The Spalding method uses a set of 70 phonograms"); Logic of English Phonogram & Spelling Rule Quick Reference.

Why the numbers differ

Three things move the count. Whether a team earns its own card: is tch a phonogram, or t plus ch? Spalding includes dge but not tch; some programs include both. How rare is too rare: eigh is in Spalding's 70, while very rare spellings like augh are left to be taught as word families. Silent letters: kn, gn and wr are phonograms in Spalding; other programs teach them as "silent letter" spellings instead.

For a child, the number matters much less than knowing each phonogram's sounds cold. A child who can say all the sounds of the first 40 or so can read most of what they'll meet in early books.

Questions parents ask

How many phonograms are there in English?

There's no single official number. The Spalding method teaches 70, Logic of English 75 basic phonograms (plus 48 advanced), and other programs use slightly different counts.

How many phonograms does Spalding have?

70: the 26 single letters, then 44 letter teams such as sh, ee, igh and eigh, taught in a fixed order.

How many sounds are there in English?

About 44 sounds (phonemes), depending on accent. Phonograms are the letters and letter teams that write those sounds.
